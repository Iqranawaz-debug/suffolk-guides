# suffolk-guides

Plain, current how-to guides for the things every Suffolk Law student has to do: booking a room, filing an SBA budget form, getting research accounts, registering for classes, and more.

Also here:

- **Calendar feeds** you add once to Google or Outlook: add/drop and exam dates, MPRE windows, LITSA events.
- **`cf-kit/`**, the character & fitness kit: a spreadsheet template for tracking addresses, jobs, legal matters and documents for your bar application, with a gap checker and a law-school-application check. The template is generated by a script; your filled-in copy stays with you.

These guides are student-written and unofficial. Each one links to the official source.
